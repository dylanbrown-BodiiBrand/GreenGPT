# GreenGPT Advisory — EHS Contractor SOW & RACI

**One-pager for contracting EHS delivery support**  
Company: GreenGPT Advisory · Product: Managed EHS Workspace  
Roles: **Principal** (GreenGPT Advisory) · **Contractor** (EHS professional) · **Client** (facility / EHS owner)

---

## 1. Purpose

Contractor provides **human EHS judgment and delivery** for assigned facilities. GreenGPT Advisory provides commercial relationship, scoping, provisioning, and the software workspace. The **Client remains the final decision owner**. Outputs are **drafts until reviewed/approved**. This is **not legal advice**.

## 4. Contractor operating rhythm (managed)

1. Check **Overview**: due soon, overdue CAPs, missing evidence, awaiting review.  
2. Update **Obligations** / calendar items; keep review states honest (`draft` → `reviewed` → `approved`).  
3. Run **Inspections** prep when walkdowns or agency visits approach.  
4. Maintain **Actions** and **Evidence** to closure standards agreed with Client.  
5. Use **Ask GreenGPT** only against approved sources; if unsupported, decline / flag — do not invent citations.  
6. Generate **Briefing** draft → human review → share only after approved.  
7. Confirm material changes appear in **Audit**.

---

## 5. Non‑negotiable rules

- GreenGPT **assists**; it does **not** replace Client EHS accountability.  
- **Commercial intake** is the web form only — do not require a completed ARDR to request a diagnostic.  
- During the **paid diagnostic**, ARDR is the **required working worksheet** (structured input). After load, the **workspace** is the system of record for status, evidence, and CAPs.  
- Post-diagnostic **ARDR is an export / handoff format** — do not chase live work only in the spreadsheet.  
- AI output is **never** auto-approved.  
- No unsourced regulatory assertions.  
- No legal advice; complex legal questions → Client counsel.  
- Stay inside assigned **scope** (facilities, document set, cadence).  
- Confidentiality: Client data / redacted packs stay in the engagement; no cross-client reuse without written approval.  
- Represent GreenGPT Advisory professionally; commercial commitments only from Principal.
