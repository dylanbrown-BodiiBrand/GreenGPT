## Slide 4 — Proposed Solution

**Title:** A dedicated AAA EHS workspace — secure, site-aware, human-reviewed

**Purpose:** Define what the thing is in one slide. Explicitly not a SaaS platform or chatbot.

**Key bullets:**

## Slide 10 — Governance and Human Oversight

**Title:** Your team stays the decision owner

**Purpose:** Address the "can we trust AI outputs" concern head-on. This slide carries the credibility of the whole deck.

**Key bullets:**
- Every output moves through **draft → review → approved** states
- AAA's EHS team remains the decision owner — the workspace recommends, people decide
- Responses are **source-linked** to the approved documents they draw from
- **No unsourced regulatory claims** — if it isn't in the approved knowledge base, the workspace flags the gap instead of improvising
- **Role-based access** — facility managers, EHS staff, and leadership see what's appropriate to their role
- **Audit trail** for generated outputs — who prompted, what sources were used, who approved
- Clear boundary: this is compliance support, **not legal advice**, and not autonomous compliance decision-making

**Speaker notes:**
Spend time here. Jason has built inspection and compliance programs where accountability matters, and a tool that blurs accountability is a liability. The design principle: the workspace makes EHS professionals faster, and never replaces their judgment. The audit trail also helps internally — you can show how any briefing or checklist was produced.
