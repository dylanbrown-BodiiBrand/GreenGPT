# Executive Summary — AI-Powered EHS Compliance Workspace
**For:** AAA Mountain West Group — Environment, Health & Safety
**From:** GreenGPT Advisory | Dylan Brown | dylanbrown416@gmail.com
**Status:** Discussion draft — pilot proposal

## The concept

GreenGPT Advisory would build a **dedicated, secure AI EHS workspace for AAA Mountain West Group** — a site-aware knowledge instance fed exclusively with AAA's own approved EHS materials: site profiles, safety plans, inspection templates, CERS/HMBP/SPCC documents, CUPA/BAR compliance notes, permits, training materials, internal standards, and corrective action logs.

AAA's EHS team, facility managers, and compliance leaders would prompt directly into the workspace and receive draft, source-linked outputs: inspection prep packets, corrective action trackers, evidence checklists, obligation registers, manager training summaries, and monthly compliance briefings.

This is not an outside firm managing compliance behind the scenes, and it is not another SaaS platform or general-purpose chatbot. It is an internal support layer that makes AAA's existing EHS knowledge faster to retrieve, convert into action, and keep consistent across sites — with AAA's EHS professionals in control at every step.

## Why it fits

Multi-site EHS programs carry recurring complexity: HMBP filings, SPCC obligations, CUPA inspections, BAR requirements, internal standards. The knowledge to handle all of it already exists inside AAA — but it lives across plans, permits, spreadsheets, SharePoint, emails, and individual experience. The workspace closes the gap between having the knowledge and applying it consistently at every site.

## Guardrails

- Every output moves through draft → review → approved states; the EHS team remains the decision owner
- Responses are source-linked to approved documents; no unsourced regulatory claims
- Role-based access and an audit trail for generated outputs
- Compliance support only — not legal advice, not autonomous compliance decisions
- Dedicated instance with client data segregation; pilot can run entirely on redacted or non-sensitive documents

## Proposed pilot (30 days)

**Scope:** 1–3 sites or one compliance workflow (e.g., inspection prep or corrective action follow-up).
**Inputs:** inspection template, sample site profile, redacted inspection findings, one HMBP/SPCC/permit-related document set.
**Deliverables:** searchable EHS knowledge instance; 10–15 validated example prompts; sample corrective action tracker; sample inspection prep checklist; sample monthly compliance briefing.
**Success criteria:** reduced time to find answers; faster conversion of findings into actions; clearer evidence requirements; improved consistency across sites.
**Commercial:** fixed-fee pilot with defined deliverables; ongoing support, if warranted, scoped by sites, users, and workflows.

## Recommended next step

Align on one pilot workflow, identify safe/redacted source documents, and review a working sample instance with the EHS team within 30 days — then decide whether to expand, with evidence in hand.
