# 10 Smart Questions for Jason Gyori

Use before sending the deck (to sharpen it) or after (to scope the pilot).

1. **Workflow priority:** Of inspection prep, corrective action follow-up, CUPA/HMBP readiness, SPCC review, manager training, and monthly briefings — which one, if it worked well, would save your team the most time this quarter?

2. **Current pain point:** When a facility inspection surfaces findings today, what happens between the finding and the closed corrective action — and where does that process stall most often?

3. **Document reality:** Where do your inspection templates, HMBPs, SPCC plans, and corrective action logs actually live today (SharePoint, shared drives, individual machines), and how current are they?

4. **Site variability:** How much do sites differ in their plans, permits, and hazmat profiles — and is cross-site consistency something you're actively trying to improve?

5. **Users:** Who would actually prompt into the workspace — your EHS staff only, or facility managers too? Roughly how many people in each group?

6. **Pilot data comfort:** What documents could you share for a pilot without needing a lengthy internal approval — and what would need redaction or sign-off first?

7. **Security gatekeepers:** Who at AAA (IT, security, legal, procurement) would need to review a tool like this before real data goes in, and what does that process usually look like?

8. **Success measurement:** How would you personally judge whether a 30-day pilot worked — time saved, output quality, team adoption, something else?

9. **Prior attempts:** Has AAA tried compliance software, AI tools, or consultants for any of this before? What worked, what didn't, and why?

10. **Budget path:** If the pilot succeeds, how does something like an ongoing EHS support engagement typically get funded and approved within MWG — your budget, a shared services budget, or an annual planning cycle?
